Areas of practice

What is going on with you

The areas below are doors of recognition, not labels. The list does not cover everything: if you do not see yourself here, you are welcome too.

Migration and life transitions

Moving countries changes everything, including what no one sees. The longing, the feeling of starting from zero, the language that is not yet yours. If your move brought a weight you did not expect, it is possible to talk about it.

Can immigration affect mental health?

Yes. Starting over in another country often comes with grief, anxiety and loneliness, even when the move was chosen and went well. Talking about it helps you get through.

Shall we talk?

Trauma

Some things we live through stay present long after they are over. The body stays on alert, sleep changes, certain memories return uninvited. It is possible to talk about it at your own pace, without having to tell everything at once.

Shall we talk?

Substance and behavioural addiction

Sometimes what began as relief becomes a weight. It may be alcohol or other substances, it may be gambling. There is no judgement here. There is a space to understand what it represents in your life and to look for other paths.

Shall we talk?

Physical health with emotional impact

A pain that will not go away, a hard diagnosis, a body that has changed. That weighs on the inside too. If what happens in your body is affecting your mood, your sleep or your routine, it is worth talking.

Shall we talk?
