Frequently asked questions

What is Narrative Therapy?

It is a form of therapy that understands we are not our problems. Together, we look at the story you tell about your life and make room to rewrite the parts that weigh on you.

Which languages does Tiago work in?

Portuguese and English, in person or online. You choose the language in which you feel most comfortable talking about yourself.
